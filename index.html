    Operating rules

    What prevents fragmentation

    The model works with existing boards. It strengthens the thread between them instead of replacing their authority.

    01

    Identity never changes

    The same AIR-ID appears on every assessment, board paper, decision and evidence reference.

    02

    Conditions travel forward

    Each recipient sees outstanding actions, accountable owners and due dates, not only “approved”.

    03

    Evidence stays authoritative

    The Case Log points to controlled evidence rather than creating contradictory copies.

    04

    Change creates re-entry

    Material change reopens the relevant gates under the existing case instead of bypassing history.
