    02 / KEEP RECORDS DISTINCT

    One thread. Clear record ownership.

    One Council-issued AIR-ID remains the system identity in AIG-INV-04 across the case history; each materially different use has its own UC-ID and separately considered priority and risk. A system-level status does not establish a decision for any use. Every use-specific decision and any conditions must be verified against authoritative UC-specific records; without that evidence, the UC decision remains unverified, including for ordinary non-agentic use. References connect separate authoritative records: AIG-DEC-04 holds plans, dated events and event-linked conditions; formal decisions stay in AIG-DEC-03 or approved minutes. AIG-INV-05 is only a descriptive controlled artefact in the proposed catalogue—it is not adopted. No record is merged, shared or transferred by this walkthrough.
